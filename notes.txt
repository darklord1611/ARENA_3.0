# What is mechanistic interpretability?
- Interpretability? -> study of understanding the models, how they work, why do they work that way?
- Mechanistic? -> the weights, activations, architectures, the "internal" things of a model

- Different from other directions like black box interpretability(not focus on weights and activations, solely on the input and output of a model), model internals -> utilize the internals for other purposes like steering vectors(make the model response in a particular manner)

- Why do this? Many goals but mine is to make AI-system safer, also it seems like lots of fun to do some partial reverse engineering how a model works

- Do more post-mortem analysis after a small project
